Ⅰ概述与基本信息Overview & Facts

边境牧羊犬源于英格兰与苏格兰交界地带,数百年来经由对工作能力而非外貌的严格选育而成,是当今公认智力最高、工作驱力最强的犬种。

The Border Collie arose along the England–Scotland border, shaped over centuries by selection for working ability rather than appearance. It is widely regarded as the most intelligent and work-driven of all breeds.

Ⅱ外观与生理特征Physical Characteristics

  • 体型——中等,体长略大于肩高,肌肉匀称,兼具速度与耐力。
  • 被毛——双层结构,外层防水、底层保暖;分长毛与短毛两型。
  • 毛色——以黑白为主,另有蓝白、红白、蓝色、三色及陨石色(merle)。
  • 眼睛——椭圆,眼神专注;部分个体为蓝眼或异色瞳。
  • 耳朵——半立或直立,随警觉度变化。
品种标志:黑白被毛与标志性的凝视姿态(the "eye")——后者是其控制羊群的本能表现,详见第 Ⅴ 章。
  • Build — medium; slightly longer than tall, muscular, built for speed and endurance.
  • Coat — double layered, weather-resistant outer and insulating under; rough or smooth.
  • Colour — chiefly black-and-white; also blue-and-white, red-and-white, blue, tricolour, merle.
  • Eyes — oval and intensely focused; some blue or heterochromatic.
  • Ears — semi-erect to erect, varying with alertness.
Breed hallmark: the black-and-white coat and the fixed stare (the "eye") — an instinct for controlling stock, discussed in §Ⅴ.

Ⅳ运动、训练与护理Exercise, Training & Care

运动

每日需 1–2 小时高强度活动(奔跑、衔取、敏捷)。单纯散步不足以满足需求;运动匮乏是绝大多数行为问题的根源。

训练

宜尽早开展社会化与基础服从,采用正向强化为主(食物、玩具、口头奖励),避免高压惩罚。

护理

长毛型每周梳理 2–3 次,换毛期加密;约每月洗浴一次;定期清耳、刷牙、修甲。

Exercise

1–2 hours of vigorous daily activity (running, retrieving, agility). Walking alone is insufficient; under-exercise underlies most behaviour problems.

Training

Begin socialisation and basic obedience early, led by positive reinforcement (food, toys, praise); avoid harsh punishment.

Grooming

Brush rough coats 2–3×/week, more in moult; bathe ~monthly; clean ears, brush teeth, trim nails regularly.

Ⅴ行为与叫声解读Behaviour & Vocalisation

解读任何单一动作前,应先判断其整体基调——同一动作(如摇尾)在放松与紧张状态下含义相反。

Before reading any single gesture, assess the overall state — the same act (e.g. tail-wagging) means opposite things when relaxed versus stressed.

Never punish a growl. A growl is a courteous warning; punishing it teaches the dog to "skip the warning and bite". Instead, remove the trigger and desensitise gradually.

5.3 边牧特有本能行为

5.3 Breed-specific instincts

  • 凝视—潜行—追逐(eye · stalk · chase)——压低身体死盯移动物,是牧羊本能链条,而非攻击。
  • 咬后跟(heeling)——本能地驱赶羊群,易由奔跑的人、车、儿童触发;宜引导至玩具。
  • 圈拢转圈——试图把人或宠物"聚成一群"。
  • 声/光高度敏感——对吸尘器、摩托、闪光反应强烈,需脱敏。
务必避免:用激光笔、逗弄影子进行游戏。光点"永远无法捕获",极易诱发难以逆转的强迫行为。
  • Eye · stalk · chase — crouching and fixating on moving objects; a herding sequence, not aggression.
  • Heeling / nipping — an instinct to drive stock, triggered by runners, bikes, children; redirect to toys.
  • Circling to gather — attempting to herd people or pets "into a flock".
  • Sound/light sensitivity — strong reactions to vacuums, engines, flashes; requires desensitisation.
Avoid laser pointers and shadow games. An uncatchable dot readily triggers hard-to-reverse compulsive behaviour.

Ⅵ健康与遗传问题Health & Genetics

  • 髋关节发育不良(CHD)
  • 眼部疾病:柯利眼异常(CEA)、进行性视网膜萎缩(PRA)
  • 癫痫
  • MDR1 基因缺陷——对部分药物敏感
  • 听力障碍(陨石色个体风险较高)
建议:定期体检;引入幼犬前核查父母犬的健康档案与基因检测(尤其 MDR1、CEA)。
  • Canine hip dysplasia (CHD)
  • Eye disorders: Collie Eye Anomaly (CEA), Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA)
  • Epilepsy
  • MDR1 mutation — sensitivity to certain drugs
  • Hearing loss (higher risk in merle individuals)
Advice: schedule regular check-ups; before acquiring a pup, verify the parents' health records and genetic tests (esp. MDR1, CEA).

Ⅶ饲养适配性评估Suitability Assessment

适合的饲主

Well suited

  • 生活方式活跃、能保证陪伴
  • 愿意投入训练与互动
  • 有意参与犬类运动(敏捷、飞盘)
  • Active lifestyle with time to spend
  • Willing to invest in training
  • Interested in dog sports

不建议的情形

Not advisable

  • 长期无人在家
  • 无法保证每日运动量
  • 期望安静低需求的伴侣犬
  • Home empty for long hours
  • Cannot ensure daily exercise
  • Wants a quiet, low-need companion
