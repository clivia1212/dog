外观特征Appearance

  • 体型:中等,身体略长,肌肉发达,兼具速度与耐力。
  • 被毛:长毛(粗毛)与短毛(顺毛)两种,双层被毛,防水保暖。
  • 毛色:黑白最常见,另有蓝白、红白、蓝色、三色、陨石色等。
  • 眼睛:椭圆形,眼神专注,部分为蓝眼或异色瞳。
  • 耳朵:半立耳或立耳,警觉时竖起。
🐾 最经典的形象是黑白被毛和标志性的"凝视"(eye)——这是它控制羊群的本能。
  • Body: Medium, slightly longer than tall, muscular — built for speed and endurance.
  • Coat: Rough (long) or smooth (short); double coat, weather-resistant and insulating.
  • Colors: Black-and-white most common; also blue-and-white, red-and-white, blue, tricolor, merle.
  • Eyes: Oval and intensely focused; some blue or heterochromatic.
  • Ears: Semi-erect or erect, raised when alert.
🐾 Its signature look: a black-and-white coat and the intense "eye" it uses to control livestock.

性格特点Temperament

  • 极其聪明 — 可理解上百个指令,训练上限极高。
  • 精力旺盛 — 需要大量运动和脑力消耗。
  • 忠诚黏人 — 渴望陪伴与协作。
  • 敏感警觉 — 对主人情绪与指令高度敏感。
  • 牧羊本能 — 会追逐、围拢移动的物体。
  • Highly intelligent — understands hundreds of commands.
  • High energy — needs lots of physical & mental work.
  • Loyal & affectionate — craves companionship and teamwork.
  • Sensitive & alert — very responsive to your cues.
  • Herding instinct — may chase and "gather" moving things.

运动 · 训练 · 护理Exercise · Training · Grooming

运动

  • 每天至少 1–2 小时高强度运动:跑步、飞盘、捡球、敏捷训练。
  • 仅散步远远不够,运动不足易焦虑、拆家。

训练

  • 尽早社会化与基础训练。
  • 以正向奖励为主(零食、玩具、表扬)。

护理

  • 长毛每周梳 2–3 次,换毛季加勤。
  • 约每月洗澡 1 次;定期清耳、刷牙、剪指甲。

Exercise

  • At least 1–2 hours of vigorous activity daily: running, frisbee, fetch, agility.
  • Walks alone aren't enough — too little leads to anxiety and destruction.

Training

  • Socialize and train early.
  • Use positive reinforcement (treats, toys, praise).

Grooming

  • Brush rough coats 2–3×/week; more in shedding season.
  • Bathe ~monthly; clean ears, brush teeth, trim nails regularly.

常见健康问题Common Health Concerns

  • 髋关节发育不良(CHD)
  • 眼部疾病(柯利眼异常 CEA、进行性视网膜萎缩 PRA)
  • 癫痫
  • MDR1 基因缺陷(对某些药物敏感)
  • 听力问题(陨石色个体更需留意)
🩺 定期体检;购买/领养时了解父母犬健康与基因检测情况。
  • Canine hip dysplasia (CHD)
  • Eye disorders (CEA, PRA)
  • Epilepsy
  • MDR1 gene mutation (drug sensitivity)
  • Hearing issues (especially merle dogs)
🩺 Get regular check-ups; ask about the parents' health and genetic tests.
